Why a coffee date club?

Learning English in Malta is easier when you actually get to practice using it. While classrooms teach the grammar, conversations are where what is learnt in class really gets the chance to be put into practice, and what better way than over a cup of coffee?

Coffee Date Club is a community where anyone who wants to practice their English and make new friends is welcome, regardless of how good your English might be at the moment. We pick a local cafe, you show up, and we talk.

What a meet-up looks like

We pick a cafe

Each gathering is held at a different coffee shop in Malta that will be announced in advance.

You show up

Any level of English is welcome! Whether you are an A1 beginner or an advanced speaker. You can come alone or bring a friend, whatever is most comfortable.

Make new friends

Activities and conversation starters to help you practice English and leave with new friends.

Hi, I’m Nicole!

I’m an English teacher in Malta, and I’ve made friends with language students and foreigners from all over the world who are learning English. I love making new friends, learning about different cultures, and most of all, having the chance to practise speaking English and make special memories while doing it.

That’s why I started my own English-speaking club: a community where people can make new friends and gain confidence, not only in their English but also in themselves, while making fun memories and supporting local businesses.
